# Demo image prompts

Use these to make start/end photo pairs in **Gemini** (image editing, "Nano Banana") or **ChatGPT**. Both can edit an image they just made while keeping the same person and room. That consistency is what makes the two frames read as one movement.

## How to make each pair

1. Start a new chat for each exercise. Paste the **Start** prompt.
2. When you like the result, reply in the same chat with the **End** prompt. It edits that image instead of starting over.
3. Download both and save them as:
   - `public/demos/photos/<exercise-id>-0.jpg` (start)
   - `public/demos/photos/<exercise-id>-1.jpg` (end)
4. Tell Claude which ones you added so they get wired in. Anything missing keeps the drawing.

Landscape (16:9) works best in the demo panel. If the tool lets you pick, choose wide.

**Reject an image if it shows** a bench, box, cable machine, barbell, dip station, squat rack, or gym. It must be home gear only.

All prompts share this style line, already included in each one:
> Realistic fitness photo, bright modern home, natural daylight, the same fit man in a plain grey t-shirt and black shorts, whole body visible, sharp focus, no gym equipment, no bench, no barbell.

## Workout A

### perfect-pushup-deficit (also used for the iso-hold finisher)
**Start:** Realistic fitness photo, bright modern living room, natural daylight, a fit man in a plain grey t-shirt and black shorts, three-quarter front-side view at floor level. He is at the bottom of a push-up gripping two rotating push-up handles with round domed black bases on a yoga mat. Palms face each other (handles parallel) and his chest is lowered slightly below the height of the grips. Body in a straight plank. Whole body visible, sharp focus, no gym equipment, no bench.

**End:** Edit this image. Keep the same man, room, camera angle and lighting. Change only his position: he is now at the top of the push-up with arms fully straight, and both handles are rotated inward so his knuckles point toward each other, squeezing his chest.

### low-bar-inverted-row
**Bar angle matters:** the body must be **perpendicular** to the bar, with the bar across the chest and legs running straight out through the doorway. Reject any image where he lies along the length of the bar (parallel to it), which is a common AI mistake. The camera should look along the bar, so the bar appears end-on or nearly end-on at the door frame.

**Start:** Realistic fitness photo of a home doorway, natural daylight. Camera is inside the room beside the door frame, looking along the length of the bar, so the bar is seen nearly end-on and the man's body is shown from the side. A doorway pull-up bar is mounted low in the door frame at waist height (about 1 meter off the floor). A fit man in a plain grey t-shirt and black shorts hangs underneath it with the bar running across his chest, perpendicular to his body, overhand grip, arms fully straight. His legs extend straight out through the doorway into the next room, heels on the floor, body a rigid straight line from heels to shoulders. Whole body visible, sharp focus, no gym, no rack, no barbell.

**End:** Edit this image. Keep the same man, doorway, bar height, camera angle and lighting, and keep his body perpendicular to the bar. Change only his arms: he has pulled his lower chest up to touch the bar, elbows bent and driven back, shoulder blades squeezed, body still a straight plank with heels on the floor.

### incline-pushup-bar
**Bar angle matters:** same as the row. Shoulders square to the bar, body perpendicular to it, feet walked straight back away from the doorway. Reject images where he's sideways to the bar or has his hands on the door frame instead of the bar.

**Start:** Realistic fitness photo of a home doorway, natural daylight. Camera beside the door frame looking along the bar, so the bar is seen nearly end-on and the man is shown from the side. A doorway pull-up bar is mounted low in the door frame at waist height (the bar's lowest setting). A fit man in a plain grey t-shirt and black shorts faces the doorway doing an incline push-up: both hands on the bar slightly wider than shoulders, shoulders square to the bar, arms straight, feet walked straight back away from the door, body a straight plank angled down from the bar to his feet. Whole body visible, sharp focus, no bench, no box, no gym.

**End:** Edit this image. Keep the same man, hallway, bar, camera angle and lighting. Change only his arms: he has lowered his lower chest to touch the bar, elbows bent, body still a straight plank.
